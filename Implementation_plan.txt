6. Module 2 — Signal Engine

Now answer:

"Is this KPI movement actually meaningful?"

You need to distinguish:

normal noise
       vs
meaningful business movement

Don't simply say:

Revenue decreased 8%
→ anomaly

Instead calculate multiple signals.

Signal 1 — Relative change
MoM Change
=
(Current - Previous) / Previous
Signal 2 — Historical deviation

Compare against:

rolling mean
rolling std

For example:

z = (current - historical_mean) / std
Signal 3 — Business materiality

An 8% decrease in:

Revenue = ₹100M

is much more important than:

Revenue = ₹10K

So:

Materiality =
Magnitude × Business Impact
Final signal

Something like:

Signal Score =
0.4 × magnitude
+ 0.3 × statistical deviation
+ 0.3 × business materiality

Then:

0–0.3    Normal
0.3–0.6  Watch
0.6–0.8  Significant
0.8–1.0  Critical

This directly answers the judges' question:

How does the engine separate meaningful change from normal noise?

The core architecture to keep in your head

Don't think:

"I have to build a dashboard, chatbot, RAG, graph, backend..."

Think:

I am building one analytical engine and several interfaces to it.

The dashboard, investigation page, evidence graph, recommendation panel, and chatbot should all ultimately consume the same underlying intelligence.
